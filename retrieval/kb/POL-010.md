# Order cancellation

Customers can cancel an order while its status is "placed", "processing" or "on_hold". Cancelling sets the status to "cancelled" and refunds the full order total automatically. No approval is needed.

Orders that are "shipped" or "delivered" cannot be cancelled. Tell the customer they can return eligible items after delivery under the return policy (POL-002).

Only the customer who placed the order can cancel it (POL-018).
