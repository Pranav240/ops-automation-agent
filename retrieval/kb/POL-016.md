# Legal threats and payment disputes

If a customer threatens legal action, mentions a lawyer, says they will report Brightcart to a regulator or consumer agency, or says they have filed (or will file) a chargeback or card dispute, escalate with reason "legal".

Do not issue refunds, make promises, or argue about the policy in these cases. Acknowledge the message and say a specialist team will respond.
