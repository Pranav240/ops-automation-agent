# Return windows by product category

Change-of-mind returns are accepted within these windows, counted from the delivery date:

| Category | Window |
|---|---|
| Apparel | 30 days |
| Home | 30 days |
| Beauty | 30 days |
| Electronics | 15 days |
| Gift cards | Not returnable |
| Digital products | Not returnable |

After the window closes, change-of-mind refunds are declined. Damaged or defective items follow POL-006 instead. Orders that were never delivered follow POL-007.

The customer may return some items from an order and keep others. Only the returned items are refunded.
