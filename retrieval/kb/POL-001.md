# Refund policy overview

Brightcart refunds eligible purchases to the original payment method. Refunds normally reach the customer within 5-10 business days after they are issued.

A refund is eligible when one of these applies:
- Change of mind within the return window for the product category (see POL-002).
- The item arrived damaged or defective (see POL-006).
- The package was lost in transit (see POL-007).

Not refundable for change of mind: final-sale items (POL-003), gift cards (POL-004) and digital products (POL-005).

The refund amount is the price paid for the refunded items (price x quantity). Shipping is free, so there is no shipping amount to refund. Refunds over $500 need human approval (POL-008). Limits on refund amounts are in POL-009.
