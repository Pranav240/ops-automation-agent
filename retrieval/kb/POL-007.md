# Lost or undelivered packages

An order is treated as lost when its status is "shipped", it has no delivery date, and 14 or more days have passed since it shipped. Lost orders get a full refund of the order total.

If fewer than 14 days have passed since shipping, look up the order, share the current status, and ask the customer to wait. Do not refund yet.

Lost-order refunds over $500 still need human approval (POL-008).
