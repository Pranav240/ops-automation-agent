# Product safety incidents

Any report that a product caused injury, burns, an allergic reaction, smoke, fire, overheating, or electric shock is a safety incident.

Escalate with reason "safety" immediately. Do not issue a refund or ask the customer to return the item. Advise the customer to stop using the product.
