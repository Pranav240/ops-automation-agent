# Shipping policy

Standard shipping is free on all orders and usually arrives 3-5 business days after the order ships. Customers receive tracking by email when the order ships.

For questions about where an order is, look up the order and share its status and shipping date. If the order shipped 14 or more days ago and has not been delivered, follow POL-007.
