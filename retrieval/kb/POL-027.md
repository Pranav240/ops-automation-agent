# Electronics warranty

Electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. Defects reported more than 30 days after delivery but within the warranty period are handled by the warranty team: escalate with reason "warranty".

Warranty claims are repairs or replacements arranged by the warranty team. Agents do not issue refunds for them.
