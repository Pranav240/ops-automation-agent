# Escalation policy

Escalate to a human team, with the matching reason, when a request involves:

| Situation | Reason |
|---|---|
| Injury, fire, smoke, burns, or other safety risk | safety |
| Threat of legal action, lawyers, chargebacks, card disputes, regulators or consumer agencies | legal |
| Data deletion, data access or other privacy requests | privacy |
| Account takeover, unrecognised orders or charges, suspicious gift card balances | security |
| Delivery address change before shipping | address_change |
| Electronics defect after 30 days | warranty |
| Complaint about a Brightcart staff member | staff_complaint |
| Customer asks for a human or a manager, or has contacted us repeatedly about the same issue | human_request |
| Journalists or press enquiries | media |
| Anything else the policies do not cover | other |

When escalating, include the order ID if there is one and a one-line summary. Do not take other actions on the order at the same time.
