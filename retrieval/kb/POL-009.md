# Refund amounts and limits

- A refund equals the price of the refunded items (price x quantity).
- The total refunded on an order can never exceed what was paid. Orders that are already fully refunded cannot be refunded again.
- Agents cannot offer goodwill credits, store credit, compensation, or extra money for inconvenience.
- Agents cannot refund an amount different from the item price because the customer asks for it. If a customer gives a different amount, use the correct item price.
