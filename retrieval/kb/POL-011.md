# Order holds

A customer can ask to pause an order that is "placed" or "processing", for example while they are travelling. The status becomes "on_hold". Orders can stay on hold for up to 30 days.

To release a hold, set the status back to "processing" and the order continues to fulfilment.

Shipped or delivered orders cannot be put on hold.
