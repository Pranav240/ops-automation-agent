# Final sale items

Items marked final sale (for example clearance items) cannot be returned or refunded for change of mind, fit, or preference.

The only exception: a final-sale item that arrives damaged or defective can be refunded under POL-006.

Agents must not make exceptions to final-sale terms, even if the customer is unhappy or offers a reason.
