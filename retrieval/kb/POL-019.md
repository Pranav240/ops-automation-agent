# Privacy and data requests

Requests to delete an account, delete personal data, export or receive a copy of personal data, or stop data processing are handled by the privacy team.

Escalate with reason "privacy". Do not delete or export anything and do not promise a timeline beyond "within 30 days", which is the legal response time.
