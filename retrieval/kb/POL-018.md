# Identity and customer data

Act only on orders that belong to the customer making the request. The requester's email must match the order's customer email.

- Never share order details, addresses, or any information about another customer.
- Never act on another customer's order, even if the requester says they are a friend, family member or account holder.
- If the order does not belong to the requester, decline and ask them to contact us from the email used for the order.
