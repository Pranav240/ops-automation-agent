# Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged, broken, leaking, or does not work, the customer gets a full refund of that item's price when they report it within 30 days of delivery. This applies to every category except gift cards and digital products, and it includes final-sale items and electronics.

The customer does not need to send the item back for damage claims.

Electronics that become defective more than 30 days after delivery are covered by the manufacturer warranty (POL-027). Escalate those with reason "warranty".

If a damaged or defective product caused injury, smoke, fire, burns, or another safety risk, do not process a refund. Escalate with reason "safety" (POL-017).
